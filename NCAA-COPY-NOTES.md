# NCAA-related copy notes (for Dean) — not legal advice

These are **draft wording suggestions** for public AZPVA pages that mention Garrett Starkey’s ASU role. They are **not legal advice** and should be reviewed by Dean (and counsel if needed) before publishing sensitive claims.

## Context already on the site

- Garrett’s bio states he is ASU’s pole vault coach **and** that AZPVA clinics/memberships are **independent of ASU athletics**.
- Clinic pages describe AZPVA membership products only — no ASU roster, tryout, or recruiting language.

## Suggested public phrasings (pick one tone)

1. **Short independence line (recommended default)**  
   “Garrett Starkey also coaches pole vault at Arizona State University. Arizona Pole Vault Academy is a separate private club; AZPVA clinics are not ASU practices, tryouts, or recruiting events.”

2. **Parent-facing clarity**  
   “Families join AZPVA for club coaching at our Queen Creek facility. Enrollment at AZPVA does not create an ASU athletics relationship and is not a pathway onto the ASU team.”

3. **Staff / social-safe variant**  
   “When we mention college coaching roles, we do so as factual biography only. AZPVA does not recruit for ASU, host ASU tryouts, or sell ‘ASU team’ access through club memberships.”

## Language to avoid on AZPVA marketing

- “ASU tryout,” “make the ASU team,” “recruiting visit,” “preferred walk-on,” or implying club membership improves NCAA eligibility or ASU roster chances.
- Bundling AZPVA clinic pricing with any university athletic opportunity.
- Photos or captions that imply an official ASU camp unless it truly is one (and is labeled correctly).

## If unsure

Keep the ASU mention to one factual sentence + the independence note, and route college-program questions to the university’s published athletics contacts — not to AZPVA enrollment CTAs.
