Arizona Pole Vault Academy offers a 2-day pole vault camp where vaulters of all levels can learn and enjoy. Camp immerses athletes in a hands-on, personalized approach to every aspect of becoming the best pole vaulter they can be.

**Dates:** October 24–25, 2026  
**Location:** Arizona Pole Vault Academy · 23238 S. Via Del Arroyo, Queen Creek, AZ 85142  
**Eligibility:** Typically boys and girls ages 12–18. Exceptions may be made case-by-case.

## Pricing & what’s included

- **Commuter — $399:** Camp, lunch, and dinner.
- **Overnighter — $599:** Camp, all meals, and sleeping accommodations at the Starkey Ranch (up to 10 campers on-site). Due to travel, overnight guests may arrive/stay the day before and after camp if needed.

Spots are first-come, first-served and limited. Register ASAP based on availability.

## Schedule highlights

- **Day 1:** Camp sessions roughly **9:00am–5:30pm**. All campers are encouraged to stay for dinner and games until about **8:00pm**.
- **Day 2 (final day):** Roughly **9:00am–4:00pm**. Full itinerary provided after registration.
- Check-in before **9:00am** on day one (out-of-town overnight guests may check in the day before).

## Itinerary may include

Pole vaulting, video analysis, gymnastics, rope swing, weight training, plyometrics, nutrition, rehab/recovery, injury prevention, sport psychology, speed work, progression methodology, round-table discussions, and recreational activities.

## Camp coaches

Dean Starkey, Garrett Starkey, Nathan Hiett, and Tray Oates — AZPVA’s high-performance coaching staff.

## What to bring

Running shoes, track spikes, workout clothes, sweat pants and shirt, swimsuit, and warm/cold-weather casual clothing. Bring your own poles if you prefer — borrowing from AZPVA’s extensive fleet is included at no extra charge.

## Out-of-town athletes

Arrange airport transfers (Uber/Lyft). Camp ends at **4:00pm** on the final day; overnight guests can arrange an extra night to fit flight schedules if needed.

## Refund policy (summary)

- Once camp has started: **no refunds** (including injury, illness, or disciplinary dismissal).
- More than 30 days before camp: full refund minus a **$25** administrative fee.
- Within 30 days of camp: refund minus a **$100** late cancellation fee.
- Within 7 days of camp: **no refunds**.

## Philosophy

We stress progressive, safety-first skill development in a ranch setting — huge technical gains, new friends, and good clean fun.
