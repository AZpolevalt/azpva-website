Garrett is Performance Director/Coach at Arizona Pole Vault Academy. A Stanford graduate with a Masters in Exercise Science and a personal best of 18'5" (5.61m), he leads day-to-day training quality, athlete development, and sports-performance support at AZPVA — including session flow, pole selection support (call **(805) 835-2811** for rentals), camps, and competition prep.

Garrett also serves as the pole vault coach at Arizona State University. **AZPVA clinics and memberships are independent of ASU athletics** — joining an AZPVA clinic is not an ASU tryout, recruiting visit, or path onto the ASU team. Questions about AZPVA training should go to AZPVA staff at **(480) 766-6017**.
