Taylor is a Coach at Arizona Pole Vault Academy. A former Division I pole vaulter at the University of Kansas and Arizona State University, she was a three-time NCAA West Preliminary qualifier and placed 6th at the Pac-12 Championships, with a personal best of 14'0.5" (4.28m). A former Arizona high school state champion and state record holder, Taylor brings experience from every level of the event into her coaching. She works with developing and competitive vaulters, emphasizing approach consistency, confidence, technical fundamentals, and simple, effective cues athletes can carry into competition.
