Jill coaches developing and competitive vaulters with a steady, athlete-first approach. A World Masters Champion and Olympic Trials competitor with a personal best of 14'1" (4.32m), she focuses on confident fundamentals, clear communication, and helping athletes and families feel at home at the Queen Creek facility.
