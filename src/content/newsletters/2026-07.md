## Summer camp is almost here

One of our favorite traditions each year is hosting athletes from across the country — and even around the world — for our seasonal pole vault camps.

If you're an AZPVA club member and haven't attended one yet, we highly encourage you to join us. Camp gives us the opportunity to dive much deeper than our regular club practices.

In addition to jump sessions, we'll cover topics such as:

- Nutrition for performance
- Training principles
- Competition psychology
- Event-specific warm-ups
- Injury prevention and prehab
- Pool vaulting and specialty drills
- Technical progressions you may not experience during regular club practices

Whether you're looking to become a better vaulter, make new friends, or simply immerse yourself in the event for three days, we'd love to have you with us.

## Looking ahead: fall training

Every experienced track athlete knows the importance of fall training.

From September through December, there are no competitions to prepare for, making it the perfect opportunity to build the athletic foundation that leads to big personal bests in the spring.

Our fall training focuses on developing complete athletes through:

- Sprint mechanics and speed development
- Strength training
- Gymnastics and body control
- Mobility and injury prevention
- Technical skill development

We have a limited number of spots available for this year's Fall Training Team. If you're interested, let one of the coaches know so we can reserve your place. More information will be released soon. See [Fall Training](/fall-training).
