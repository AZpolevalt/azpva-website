## August focus: build your foundation

Every year we see athletes eager to jump from full approaches as soon as possible.

This August, we're doing the opposite.

We're taking a step back so we can take a much bigger step forward.

Throughout the month we'll focus on rebuilding every athlete's technical foundation through a progression beginning with one-left drills before gradually advancing through two-lefts, three-lefts, and beyond. This allows us to refine posture, takeoff mechanics, timing, swing, and inversion before additional speed and complexity are introduced.

The athletes who embrace this process now are often the ones who make the biggest improvements once competition season arrives.

If you've recently paused your membership, August is the perfect time to jump back in.

There is no better opportunity all year to rebuild your technique, develop better habits, and establish a strong foundation before preseason training begins.

Whether your goal is setting a personal best, qualifying for state, earning a college scholarship, or simply becoming a more confident vaulter, this is where that progress starts.

Let's build something special together.

## Looking ahead: Reno Pole Vault Summit

January may seem a long way off, but planning for the Reno Pole Vault Summit starts now.

The Summit has become the premier pole vault event in the world, bringing together thousands of athletes, coaches, Olympians, professionals, and beginners under one roof.

With nearly twenty runways operating simultaneously, educational clinics throughout the weekend, and competition flights for athletes of every ability level, there simply isn't another meet like it.

If you've never attended before, we strongly encourage making this your travel meet for the season.

**January 15–16, 2027 · Reno, Nevada**

AZPVA will once again coordinate coaching, pole transportation, and pole usage so families don't have to worry about traveling with poles. We consolidate poles into shared bags and ship them directly to Reno through Southwest Cargo, making travel significantly easier.

**AZPVA travel fee: $299.** [Reno travel details](/events/reno-summit-2027).

Includes:

- Coaching throughout the event
- Pole transportation
- Pole usage
- Logistics coordination

Athletes are still responsible for registering separately through the Reno Pole Vault Summit once registration opens in September.

If you're considering attending, now is an excellent time to begin planning flights and accommodations while costs are typically lower.

This is one of the most memorable experiences in our sport, and we'd love to have another large AZPVA group representing Arizona.
